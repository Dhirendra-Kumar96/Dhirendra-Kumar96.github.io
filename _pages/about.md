I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at **UNIST, South Korea**.

My research focuses on the theoretical understanding of electronic, spin, and optical properties of low-dimensional quantum materials using first-principles computational methods.

## Research Interests

- Two-dimensional materials and van der Waals heterostructures
- Excitons and optical properties
- Rashba spin splitting and spin–orbit physics
- Quantum materials for energy applications
- Hydrogen evolution reaction (HER)
- Oxygen evolution reaction (OER)
- Ultrafast spin dynamics
- Light–matter interactions
- Real-time time-dependent density functional theory (RT-TDDFT)
- First-principles many-body perturbation theory

## Computational Methods

My research primarily uses density functional theory (DFT) and many-body perturbation theory, including:

- DFT and DFT+U
- Spin–orbit coupling (SOC)
- GW and Bethe–Salpeter equation (GW-BSE)
- Real-time TDDFT
- Quantum ESPRESSO
- YAMBO

My doctoral research focused on:

**“Rashba and Excitonic Physics in Two-Dimensional Structures for Energy Applications.”**

I am interested in developing first-principles approaches to understand and control electronic, spin, and excited-state phenomena in emerging quantum materials.
